# MyPropertyVisit user guide — launch edition

MyPropertyVisit helps you record property visits, keep compass photos/videos together and enter room measurements manually. Capture, editing and local playback work without a MyPropertyVisit account or network connection. Sending media through another app may need a connection.

## Prepare a property

Open **Add a property** to save a property name, type and area/address. Open **Saved properties** to review existing records. Open a property's tools to create captures associated with that property. Recording directly from Home can produce unassigned captures.

## Record a visit

1. Open **Record walkthrough**, allow Camera access and hold the phone upright.
2. Keep the phone away from magnets/large metal objects. The red needle indicates magnetic north; degrees describe the phone heading.
3. Tap a zoom preset if needed. Available buttons depend on camera hardware.
4. Tap Record, then Stop to finish. Videos contain no microphone audio.
5. On iOS 18 or later, Pause enables taking a photo while the recording is paused. Resume continues the same video without the paused interval. On iOS 17, take photos between separate videos.
6. The first capture creates a visit. End session separates subsequent captures. Check the session label before continuing after leaving/reopening the recorder.

Backgrounding or a camera interruption requests recording stop/finalization. Confirm the saved result in the library before assuming the video is complete.

## Photos and room measurements

Tap Photo in the recorder or **Capture room photo** during video playback. Open the room gallery to view or edit the saved photo. Tap a photo to open it full screen, then close the viewer to return.

Choose the room deliberately. Select the same existing room to share its name, category, measurements and notes with another photo. Create separate rooms for Bedroom 1 and Bedroom 2 even though both use the Bedroom category.

Enter length and width together, or leave both empty. Height is optional. Use positive values in metres or decimal feet: 10.5 ft means 10 feet 6 inches. Area is a rectangular calculation from the entered length and width, not an automatic measurement of the actual room footprint. Verify dimensions independently.

## Share or save media

- Photos: open the photo's ellipsis menu and choose **Share or save photo**. The Room photos toolbar also opens multi-photo export.
- Video: **Share video with compass** prepares a rendered compass video. **Save video to Photos** opens the export picker.
- Mixed media/session: open **Photos & videos → Share or save media**, or open a session first and use its export option.
- All available items start selected. Deselect all to choose individual items, or use Select all to export the whole displayed collection.
- Choose **Save selected to Photos** and allow add-to-Photos permission, or **Share selected** and choose a destination.
- Wait for preparation to finish. Check the completion count. If saving partly fails, successfully saved items are deselected so a retry targets the remaining failures. Selecting them again saves another copy.

Saved files preserve the embedded compass. Room dimensions and notes remain inside MyPropertyVisit; they are not added automatically to the JPEG/video. Media exports cannot restore the complete app project. Other apps may recompress shared media or change metadata; check the received result when quality matters.

## Profile, appearance and deletion

Settings offers a local profile, an About & your data explanation and System/Light/Dark theme. The optional profile is not a verified account. **Clear local profile** clears those profile fields but keeps saved properties and media.

Delete a room photo through its menu. In Saved properties, swipe to delete a property record; associated captures may remain. This launch source does not provide a dedicated video/session deletion control. To remove the whole local app container on iOS, use Delete App rather than Offload App; export anything needed first. Copies in Photos, share destinations and device backups are separate and must be managed there.

There is no MyPropertyVisit server copy to recover deleted local data. Do not reinstall as an initial troubleshooting step.
