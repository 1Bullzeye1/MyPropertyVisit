# Owner setup and launch decisions

## Recommended starting route

MyPropertyVisit is the selected product name, approved on 10 November 2026. Confirm registration of mypropertyvisit.com and finish the name review before public release. First decide whether the seller will be you personally or an existing registered organization. A company is not required simply to publish an app as an individual.

| Route | Public seller identity | Needed from owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Individual / sole proprietor | Your legal personal name | Verified Apple Account, actual country/address, membership, tax and banking information |
| Organization | Verified legal entity name | Legal entity details, enrollment authority and generally D-U-N-S verification; functional organization website |

Choose the organization route if an established legal entity should be the seller. Do not create a fictitious company name in an individual enrollment. Apple describes the identity rules in its [enrollment guidance](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/program-enrollment/) and [D-U-N-S guidance](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/D-U-N-S/).

Apple membership is normally USD 99 per year or local currency where available. Check the enrollment screen for the actual regional charge. The app brand and legal seller name are separate fields.

## Website and mailbox

Use one domain under an owner-controlled account for the product website and a real monitored support mailbox. A professional arrangement is:

- Website: https://YOUR_DOMAIN
- Support page: https://YOUR_DOMAIN/support/
- Privacy page: https://YOUR_DOMAIN/privacy/
- Mailbox: support@YOUR_DOMAIN; privacy requests may use the same monitored inbox.

These are templates, not registered domains or functioning addresses. A dedicated mailbox on an existing domain can be used before buying a new one. Keep registrar/email ownership with the publisher, enable two-factor authentication and record renewal dates privately.

## Free domain request

No standalone, permanently free .com offer was verified. [Hostinger's first-year offer](https://www.hostinger.com/free-domain) is tied to eligible paid plans. [Cloudflare Registrar](https://domains.cloudflare.com/) sells domains at registry cost; it is not free registration. Compare registration, renewal and email costs together before choosing a provider.

The private Sites preview works without a purchased domain. It is for owner review. Before using it for public support/privacy, publish to a public audience and ensure visitors can read it without signing in. Custom-domain attachment is a later step after ownership and DNS are available.
