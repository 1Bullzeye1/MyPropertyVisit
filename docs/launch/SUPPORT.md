## Before contacting us

Include your phone model, OS version, MyPropertyVisit version/build, what you tried and what happened. A short reproduction sequence is more useful than private property footage. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or identity documents. Remove personal information from screenshots before sending them. No response-time guarantee is set for this launch.

## Camera does not open

Check Camera permission for MyPropertyVisit in device Settings. Close another app using the camera, return to MyPropertyVisit and try again. A physical device is needed to validate real camera and compass behavior; simulators may not provide these sensors.

## Compass is unavailable or looks wrong

Move away from magnets and metal, hold the phone upright and try another position. The reading is magnetic north, not a survey measurement or automatic GPS alignment. Some devices cannot supply a valid compass. Missing historical readings cannot be recreated later.

## Export does not finish or only some files save

Check free space and, on iOS, add-to-Photos permission. Keep the export screen open and check the success count. Successful saves are deselected, so retry remaining failed items. Re-selecting and saving successful items again makes additional copies. iOS video export may take time because the compass is rendered into the exported video.

On Android, look for MyPropertyVisit under Pictures for photos and Movies for videos; the Gallery app may show these as albums. A sharing destination may change file size/quality.

## Where are room dimensions in my shared file?

Dimensions and notes remain editable in MyPropertyVisit. Current exports preserve compass artwork, but do not automatically print room dimensions or notes over a photo/video. Sending a media file does not send an editable MyPropertyVisit project.

## Can I recover my records on another phone?

The launch app has no cloud account or complete project restore. Exports keep media copies, not all room/project information. Device backups and Photos synchronization are separate platform services. Do not delete/reinstall the app as a first troubleshooting step.

## Why is a call option visible?

The current working build has calling placeholders. Live video calling and invitation codes are not supported by this launch. These controls are recorded as release cleanup items and are not included in the paid feature description.

## How do I delete data?

Delete room photos through their menu and property records through available property controls. Clear the local profile in Settings. Property deletion and profile clearing do not clear all associated media. Dedicated video/session deletion controls are not present in the reviewed source. Use the device's app deletion/storage controls for the whole local app container after exporting what you need. Separately remove exported/shared copies and manage backups through their providers.
