## Description

Keep a clearer record of every property visit.

MyPropertyVisit brings property details, compass photos, silent walkthrough videos and manually entered room dimensions together on your device. Use it while viewing land, flats and buildings, then return to the details after your visit.

CAPTURE THE VISIT
Record rear-camera walkthroughs, take photos and use the zoom buttons available on your device. Photos and videos are grouped into capture sessions. On iOS 18 or later, pause a recording, take photos, then resume the same video.

KEEP DIRECTIONS VISIBLE
Save photos with an embedded compass and export walkthrough videos with a compass and degree readout. Compass readings depend on your phone's sensors and nearby conditions; missing readings are shown as unavailable.

ADD ROOM DETAILS YOURSELF
Name rooms, choose categories, and enter length, width, optional height and notes. Use metres or decimal feet. Photos assigned to the same room share its details. Dimensions are entered by you, not measured automatically.

REVIEW AND SHARE
View photos full screen, replay saved videos, and select individual items or an entire session to share or save to Photos. Exported files preserve their compass. Editable room dimensions, notes and project relationships stay in MyPropertyVisit and are not automatically printed onto media exports.

WORK OFFLINE
Capture, browse and edit locally without a MyPropertyVisit login. Choose System, Light or Dark appearance and optionally save a local profile. Sharing through another service may require a connection.

IMPORTANT
Videos are silent. Native pause/resume requires iOS 18 or later. Camera zoom and compass availability depend on your device. MyPropertyVisit has no cloud backup, project restore, live calling or automatic room measurement in this version. Save important media separately before deleting the app; exported files are not a complete project backup.

One-time paid download. No MyPropertyVisit subscription is required for these launch features.
